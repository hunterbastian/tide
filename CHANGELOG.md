# Changelog

Newest first. Dates are when the work landed in the claude.ai build.

## 2026-10-07
- **Crisp pixels, always:** the pixel filter is locked to Crisp (no dither). Saves on Off/Fine/Chunky load as Crisp, and the Pixel render button is removed from Settings. Colour grade, outlines, tilt-shift and bloom are still adjustable.
- **Mediterranean coast:** the sea goes clear turquoise over pale white sand near every shore and deepens to sapphire offshore, with dark Posidonia seagrass patches on the bed. Coastal cliffs (up to ~6 tiles inland) turn to pale layered limestone with ochre stains, weathering streaks and a wet algae line; a per-vertex `aCoast` attribute swaps the purple shadow glow for warm bounce light so they stay sunny in shade. Coastal grass dries toward olive with poppies, broom and cistus; silvery beach grass and white pebble coves. Worldgen (`W.coastD`, inland distance from the sea) places up to 10 **cypresses** in clusters on the cliff tops and 3 **stone pines**. (A whitewashed sea chapel was tried and removed.) Instanced lavender, agave and prickly pear; up to 7 limestone **sea stacks** with foam collars and ripples. The quay gets stone bollards, amphorae and an iron lantern that glows and reflects on the water at night; Odda's awning is now blue and white and the rowboat is painted like a Greek fishing boat. Inland meadows, the mountain and Frostmere are unchanged. New props only block tiles that keep everything reachable, so no world-version bump (saves standing on one move to spawn).
- **Perf:** invisible tap targets no longer cost a draw call (`INVIS.visible=false`; raycasts still hit them).
- **Frostmere Plain (tundra plateau):** worldgen raises a broad flat plain (level 4) on Cairnhold's shoulder, stepping down to the meadows in 1-level terraces so ramps connect it (`W.tundra`, `W.plateau`). Straw-gold ground with grey-green lichen and frost patches, tall pale tundra grass, heather instead of wildflowers, half-sunk boulders, at most 4 pines and no broadleaf trees. A broken ring of 4–7 standing stones (`stone` entities, faint rune glow, brighter at night; Touch makes them flare). Light snow on the plain. First visit shows a banner; stepping into the ring centre gives Agility XP. World version bumped to 4 (players return to spawn once).
- **Cinematic clip:** `tide-cinematic-x.mp4`, 22s 1080p30, four shots (wading the shallows, the waterfall, following the stream to the sea, Fern Isle on the horizon), rendered frame by frame from the real game.

## 2026-10-05
- **Grass that parts:** the grass wind shader takes 8 push points (`WIND.uTrail`): you, your pet, the nearest sheep, and your last 5 footsteps. Tufts and flowers bend away and flatten (darker "trodden" tint), and footsteps spring back with a damped wobble over ~2s. Striding through meadow kicks up green flecks.
- **Fish jumping:** fishing spots launch leaping fish every few seconds near the camera. Streamlined bodies (tapered sphere, vertex-coloured: silver with blue back, or olive trout with pink stripe + spots), forked tails, dorsal/pectoral fins, eyes, Phong sheen. Spine flex in the vertex shader (S-wave + dorsal curl), mid-air roll, under-surface shadow "tell" before each leap, droplets, sun glint at the apex, crown splash + double ripple + lingering bubbles, plop nearby. Silver fish skitter in groups of 2–3; trout make single big leaps. `JUMPERS`, `launchFish`, `updateJumpers`.
- **Butterflies v2:** five species with pixel-painted wings (monarch, cabbage white, blue morpho, swallowtail, painted lady; 32px canvas textures, alpha-tested), fore/hind wings on hinged pivots, segmented body, head, antennae with knobs, ground shadow. Flight: quick-downstroke flapping with lift, glides, wandering steering leashed to a home meadow; they visit flowers (`FLOWER_PTS`), rest with slow wing beats, and flee when you approach.
- **Chimney smoke:** the cottage has a stone chimney; soft dithered pixel puffs rise and drift from it (and from the furnace, more while smelting), greyer and fainter at night. `SMOKE`, `updateSmoke`.
- **Butterflies:** 14 butterflies (white, yellow, orange, blue, pink) flutter over meadows near trees by day and fade out at dusk when the fireflies take over. Lazily created on first update (`initButterflies`) because they look up trees in `ENTS`.
- **Fix:** title cleanup is null-safe.
- **Locked pixels:** in pixel modes the camera uses a near-orthographic long lens (5° FOV from further back, same framing) when looking down, easing back to normal perspective toward the horizon; each frame `lockPixels()` snaps the camera's translation to the pixel grid on the focal plane and slides the upscaled canvas by the leftover sub-pixel (CSS transform) so motion stays smooth. Fog, near/far planes and outline depth adjust with the lens. Rotating the camera can still shift pixels slightly.
- **Waterfalls rebuilt:** arcing sheets that leave the lip and widen as they fall (teal body + faster white foam veil, feathered edges, lit lip, pixel-snapped streaks; custom shaders sharing `STREAM_FU`), churning foam ring at the foot, softer pool, two breathing mist puffs, continuous spray and ripples. Dims at night.
- **Mountain stream + waterfalls:** worldgen traces an all-downhill route from a spring high on Cairnhold to the sea (Dijkstra, favours cliff drops, avoids props/paths; `W.stream`, `W.streamSet`). Rendered as a meandering ribbon with scrolling pixel foam and waterfall sheets at each drop (foam lip, pool, mist, splashes, ripples). Wading it slows you a little and splashes. Shown on the minimap. Typically 5–6 falls.
- **Title screen:** removed the glass arches; the painted island now shows unobstructed behind the card.
- **Painterly title screen:** the live island slowly orbits behind an oil-paint (Kuwahara) post pass with canvas weave and a muted sage/cream palette; stacked clear-glass arches, a frosted card (sun-ray glyph, TIDE, tagline) and a white pill button (Begin/Continue). Tapping it melts the paint back into the pixel game over 1.1s and returns the camera to the Wanderer; the how-to card follows on a first visit. `TITLE` state, `uPaint` uniform.
- **Chop + mine animations:** wind-up / hold / strike / recover cycle synced so the blade lands exactly on each attempt (two swings per attempt); torso twist, lean, two-handed grip, knee bend on pick strikes; wood chips, falling leaves, sparks and ore-coloured rock chips; trees shudder and rocks jolt; tiny camera jolt; new 'chop' and 'mine' sounds.
- **Faster chopping per level:** woodcutting now runs on smooth time; each level adds +0.85% swing speed (x1.83 at 99), the axe animation keeps pace, and the Skills panel shows the bonus. `chopTick(dt)` drives it from the main loop.
- **Wade narrow water:** any 1-tile-wide water (land on both sides, or a small pocket) with low banks is wadeable; from a level-1 ledge you hop down into it and climb back out (counts as a climb). Fishing spots and the dock stay deep.
- **Wanderer:** removed the eye glints; the hood is now a featureless shadow.
- **Fix: black cut-outs in the sea.** Light reflections divided by zero on the far side of each light (inf × 0 = NaN, which renders black), slicing straight-edged dark regions into the water. The streak loop now skips fragments outside each streak.
- **Fix: mist shader didn't compile** (numbers weren't inserted into the GLSL), so the mist was silently missing; fixed.
- **Fix: chopped-looking water.** The sea-mist sheets (enlarged for Fern Isle) were laying hard-edged patches across the open sea. Mist now fades out away from the main island's coast, has softer edges, and thins at night.
- **Living water:** bed refraction wobble, warm light reflections (wisp, fires, furnace, cottage windows), 80 floating leaves you push aside while wading, wet sand + moving tide wash line.
- **Crisp + Abyss look (new default):** pixel mode without ordered dither, Abyss grade (teal shadows, warm highlights, strong vignette), outlines off. Thin web caustics on a 22/unit grid, pebbled bed, clearer teal shallows, night water stays teal, 1px sparkles, dashed ripple rings.
- **Fern Isle (was the sandbar):** much larger (beach ring + grassy knoll, palms, ferns, flowers, rocks, wreck, tide pool, crabs, beachcombing), moved ~52 units offshore; distant mountain ring pushed back; faster rowing.
- **Brontosaur herd:** 3 unnamed brontosaurs (two adults + a youngster) with smooth continuous neck/tail tubes, spring-driven behaviours (idle look, graze + chew, bellow, shake, stretch, greet), diagonal wading gait with wakes, separation and boat-lane avoidance.
- **Cairnhold Peak:** taller (8 levels, snow cap), pines (Woodcutting 20, pine logs), mountain goats that hop cliffs, summit cairn with your own gold flag, ~8s Skyrim-style aura celebration (levitation, wisps, rune circle, light pillar, shockwave), snow flurries up high.
- **Smithing + coins:** furnace/anvil, bronze/iron bars, tool tiers that speed gathering and widen the fishing zone, nails, Odda's stall, eating food.
- **Three.js r128 → r170:** module loader, colour management off + linear output, light units scaled (LX / PLX), Lambert flatShading removed for parity; fixed `setFromPoints` regression in the minimap route.
- **Island 1.2× bigger** with a central mountain; voxel minimap; QA fixes (mountain ramps, stall placement fallback, landscape HUD overlaps, shadow frustum follows zoom).
